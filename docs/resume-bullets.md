## Short statement for a master's application

TradeGuard AI helped me move from treating machine learning as a standalone prediction task to treating it as one governed component of a decision system. The project forced me to connect calibration, uncertainty, reproducibility, data provenance, human review, and software architecture. It also clarified the research questions I want to pursue further: how to validate models under limited and shifting trade data, how to measure decision utility beyond discrimination, and how to design human-model workflows that remain contestable and auditable.
