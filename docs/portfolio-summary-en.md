# TradeGuard AI - portfolio summary

TradeGuard AI is an explainable risk-decision workspace for small cross-border trading businesses. I built it to connect the parts that are often fragmented across spreadsheets: counterparty onboarding, document evidence, restricted-party screening, commercial terms, deterministic rules, statistical risk estimation, human review, and audit history.

The system is a FastAPI and SQLAlchemy modular monolith with Alembic migrations, role-based access, PostgreSQL/SQLite support, a no-build responsive frontend, and downloadable PDF reports. Its ML pipeline generates a versioned public-safe synthetic dataset, compares calibrated logistic regression and random forest candidates, selects a champion using held-out calibration and discrimination metrics, verifies artifact checksums at inference, and routes out-of-range inputs to review. Portfolio analytics keep currencies separate and persist reproducible, non-decisioning stress-test snapshots. Approvals with conditions create owned, due-dated obligations that require linked transaction evidence to fulfill and surface in data-quality monitoring when overdue. Structured KYB facts require source evidence, human verification, validity dates, version checks, and immutable assessment snapshots. Versioned counterparty credit policies add explicit currency limits, term caps, deposit floors, projected-exposure checks, and internal restricted-credit stops.

The most important product choice is separation of authority: a model score can inform commercial-risk review, but it cannot clear a compliance hold. Entity-resolution similarity also remains a human-review signal rather than an automatic merge. Every assessment stores the input snapshot, list versions, rule checksum, model version, prediction features, reasons, recommendation, and reviewer action.

This project demonstrates full-stack engineering, applied ML, model governance, product judgment, testing, and clear communication about limitations. It is a portfolio system, not a claim that synthetic model performance transfers to real credit decisions.
