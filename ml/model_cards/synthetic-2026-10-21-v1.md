# TradeGuard synthetic credit-risk model card

Model version: `synthetic-2026-10-21-v1`

## Intended use

This model is a portfolio demonstration of a payment-risk workflow. It predicts the synthetic target described below and may be used only with public-safe demonstration data.

**Target:** Synthetic indicator that a trade invoice becomes at least 90 days past due or produces a material payment loss within 180 days after its contractual due date.

It must not be used for lending, credit denial, sanctions decisions, legal compliance, or real family-business limits.

## Data

The training dataset is generated by code with a fixed seed. It contains no real companies, people, invoices, payment histories, or confidential business records.

- `transaction_document_count`
- `counterparty_evidence_count`

## Calibration and uncertainty

Both candidates use sigmoid calibration with cross-validation. Inference checks each feature against the recorded training range. Out-of-range input is marked low confidence and routed to review.

## Limitations

- Synthetic relationships can make performance look better than real deployment performance.
- No subgroup fairness claim can be made because protected attributes are neither generated nor used.
- No temporal validation or real economic-cycle stress test has been performed.
- Reasons returned by the API are policy-oriented feature flags, not causal explanations.
- Retraining on real outcomes requires a separate data-governance, privacy, validation, and approval process.
